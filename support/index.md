## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I buy SaturnVex products?**
Contact {{ site.email }} for availability, pricing and orders.

**Can I use the example firmware in my own product?**
Yes. SaturnVex example source files are provided under the BSD-3-Clause license; keep the copyright notices and follow the
third-party licenses listed in each project's `NOTICE.txt`.

**Do you offer custom hardware or firmware development?**
Yes: board design, firmware, motor control and Edge AI integration based on these platforms or new designs. Send your requirements by email.

**The PDF guides cannot be edited. Is that intended?**
Yes. Guides are published read-only; they can be viewed, printed and copied.
