SaturnVex™ is an embedded engineering brand. It designs development platforms that combine
hardware, firmware and on-device intelligence, and publishes every product with tested example projects, measured results and CAD data.

## Focus areas

- **Hardware**: MCU kits, processor and FPGA SoC boards, multi-axis motor controllers and power modules.
- **Firmware**: example projects for TI C2000, STM32, ESP32-S3, Raspberry Pi and FPGA SoC, with step-by-step guides.
- **Edge AI**: on-device intelligence on ESP32, Raspberry Pi, FPGA SoC and other platforms for a wide range of applications.

## Capabilities

- **Hardware design**: schematic and PCB layout in KiCad and Altium, simulation and bench validation.
- **Firmware & programming**: STM32, TI C2000 and ESP32 firmware; embedded Linux and PetaLinux.
- **FPGA SoC**: AMD Xilinx designs with Vivado, Vitis and Vitis HLS.
- **UAV/Drone**: flight controllers, ESC motor drives, video processing, power and navigation modules.
